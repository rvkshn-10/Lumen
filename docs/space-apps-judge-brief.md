## One pitch for the panel

“Near the lunar south pole, the local skyline determines how long a mission can see Earth. In our October 5–12 example, both sites receive sunlight throughout the window. Site A keeps Earth in view about 14 hours longer. We can replay the cutoff and inspect the NASA terrain and JPL geometry behind it. Our optional OpenVINO model shows where a small visibility model agrees and where its simplifications matter.”
